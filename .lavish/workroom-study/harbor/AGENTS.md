# Evidence-led review guidance

Reconstruct the current system from the supplied evidence before recommending a direction.

Restate the underlying decision in plain language so a misunderstanding is visible early. Separate observed behavior, source claims, proposals, accepted decisions, and unknowns. Trace both what the mechanism does and why its authority boundaries exist.

Do not accept the first proposed design by default. Consider the smallest plausible ways to answer the main uncertainty, and prefer a cheap observation or disposable prototype when it can replace abstract debate. Define what evidence would change the decision.

Verify every material conclusion against the supplied sources. State the practical limit of the evidence. Keep the result concise and readable.
