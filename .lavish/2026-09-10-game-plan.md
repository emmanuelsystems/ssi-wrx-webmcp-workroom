## 3. Reassess the proposed next WRX experiment

I’ll examine whether the four blinded reconstruction cards address an important uncertainty in WRX. The useful question is whether an evaluator can catch an account that turns a proposal into an accepted decision or an unsupported dependency into fact, while still recognizing faithful accounts.

This is a diagnostic smoke test. Passing would not establish general reliability, independent verification, reusable workflow learning, or reduced human review effort. I also need to distinguish a failure of the evaluator from ambiguity in the source or disagreement in the human reference labels.

I’ll now compare two possible experiment shapes: a document-level four-card smoke test and a small disposable Workroom prototype that makes the same distortions inspectable in the product flow. The choice should depend on which one answers the uncertainty with the least added machinery.

**Output:** recommend keeping, revising, prototyping, or deferring the test, with a reason tied to the next WRX decision. Suggested time: 30 minutes.

## 4. Prepare one bounded experiment brief

If the test still looks useful, I’ll specify one accessible internal handoff, who will label it, a source-based rubric, the preparer/evaluator separation, and the exact result that would change our next step. David and I would need to resolve or preserve label disagreements before scoring an evaluator. The answer key must stay outside the evaluator’s context.

The initial screen would use two faithful accounts and two accounts with consequential distortions. Catching both defects and correctly accepting both faithful accounts would justify a larger held-out evaluation. Either missed defect or indiscriminate rejection would direct us to examine the source labels, evidence representation, and evaluation rules first. Four cards alone would not justify architecture or efficiency claims.

**Output:** a reviewable brief with the source, roles, rubric, success/failure criteria, missing prerequisites, and the scoped authorization needed before running it. If a source or human labels are unavailable, record that gap; do not present invented labels as a valid reference. Suggested time: 30 minutes.
