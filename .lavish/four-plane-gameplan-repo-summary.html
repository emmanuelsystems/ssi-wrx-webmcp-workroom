CURRENT IMPLEMENTATION

Strong governed baseline

  • Episodes are local-first and persist in browser localStorage, while source originals and extracted text remain in IndexedDB.
  • Agents can contribute inspectable evidence, proposals, evaluations, and node-thread responses.
  • Only humans accept structure, advance workflow stages, promote trusted context, or record a final disposition.
  • Loopback Codex runs are bounded, read-only, approval-free, network-disabled, and constrained by maximum-turn policies.
DESIGN INTENT

Make the existing boundaries legible

The redesign is not a replacement of governance. It turns existing capabilities into explicit planes so that a participant can answer, at every point: what was said, what is allowed, what agents are doing, and what evidence still needs a human judgment.

What changes—and what does not

Governance plane

Expose authority, consent, constraints, and checkpoints consistently across the workroom.
